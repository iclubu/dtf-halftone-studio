How DTF works: export one transparent sRGB PNG at 300 DPI, unmirrored. The RIP converts to CMYK+white, builds the white underbase from the PNG's transparency, mirrors for film and screens tone itself. Halftone fades only: solid areas stay solid. The separate underbase file is white exactly under the ink, choked at the outer edge only. Every pixel is either fully on or fully off. Nothing leaves your browser.
